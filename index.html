🏛️ About Drecot Consults

Drecot Consults is a visionary parent company that provides strategic direction, operational support, and brand development to its subsidiaries. We are committed to building businesses that create lasting impact and drive positive change across the African continent.

Through Kwashate, we celebrate and honour excellence in the hospitality and tourism industry. Through HospitalityRecruit, we connect top talent with leading hospitality organizations.

Our mission is to increase the level of comfort and loyalty of guests, to raise demand for hospitality industry services and related spheres, and to contribute to the economic growth of the nation.
