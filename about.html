About Drecot Consults

The parent company of Kwashate and HospitalityRecruit – building excellence in events, hospitality, and recruitment across Africa.

🏛️ Who We Are

Drecot Consults is a visionary parent company that provides strategic direction, operational support, and brand development to its subsidiaries. We are committed to building businesses that create lasting impact and drive positive change across the African continent.

Through Kwashate, we celebrate and honour excellence in the hospitality and tourism industry. Through HospitalityRecruit, we connect top talent with leading hospitality organizations.

Our goal is to raise the standard of service across Nigeria by providing training, staffing, and recognition where it is needed most.

🎯 Our Mission

To increase the level of comfort and loyalty of guests, to raise demand for hospitality industry services and related spheres, and to contribute to the economic growth of the nation.

We achieve this by:

  • 🏆 Recognizing excellence through prestigious awards like Kwashate.
  • 🤝 Providing trained professionals to hotels and event centers.
  • 📈 Consulting with businesses and governments to develop tourism strategies.
  • 🎓 Offering certification programs to upskill the next generation of hospitality leaders.
