About Drecot Consults

Building the future of Nigeria's hospitality industry, one partnership at a time.

🏛️ Who We Are

Drecot Consults is a premier hospitality support firm dedicated to developing the hospitality and tourism industry in Nigeria. Starting from Kwara State, we have expanded our reach by setting high standards through state-level Hospitality Awards, professional staffing, and strategic consulting.

We are a team of industry professionals who understand that the heart of hospitality is people—both the guests and the staff who serve them. Our goal is to raise the standard of service across Nigeria by providing training, staffing, and recognition where it is needed most.

🎯 Our Mission

To increase the level of comfort and loyalty of guests, to raise demand for hospitality industry services and related spheres, and to contribute to the economic growth of the nation.

We achieve this by:

  • 🏆 Recognizing excellence through prestigious awards like KWASHATE.
  • 🤝 Providing highly trained professional staff to hotels and event centers.
  • 📈 Consulting with businesses and governments to develop tourism strategies.
  • 🎓 Offering certification programs to upskill the next generation of hospitality leaders.
